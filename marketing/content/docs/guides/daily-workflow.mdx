**Today** (sidebar, hotkey `G` `D`) is the trading day on one page: the routine that opens it,
the live desk while it runs, what happened, what you passed on, and the log that closes it.
`/today` always resolves to the current trading day in your **market timezone**, so a New York
session viewed from Asia stays one day.

The sidebar is kept short — **Home, Today, Trades, Calendar, Reports**, then **Notes, Playbook,
Import**. Routines and Missed trades are sections of Today; their full pages are one click away
from each card, or from the command palette (`⌘K` → *Routines* / *Missed trades*).

## What's on Today

Top to bottom:

| Card | Shows |
|------|-------|
| **This week's focus** | The 1–3 commitments from your last [weekly review](/en/docs/guides/weekly-review) |
| **Routine** | Today's checklist, grouped by stage; **Edit** opens the routine editor |
| **Daily loss limit** | How much of today's max daily loss is spent — amber from 70%, red when hit. Only shown once **Max daily loss** is set under Settings → Rules |
| **Open positions** | Everything on the book now (whenever it was opened), the total open risk from recorded stops, and how many positions have **no stop** |
| **Session summary** | Net P&L, trades, win rate, fees; rule-break and behaviour pills (over-risked, revenge, oversized…); "N trades to review →"; the day's mistake tax |
| **Intraday P&L** | Cumulative realized P&L, stepped at each close |
| **Trades** | The day's trades — click one to open its drawer |
| **Missed trades** | Setups you saw and didn't take that day; **All** opens the full log |
| **Daily log** | The day's notes, with **New note** |

The arrows step to other days (`/day/YYYY-MM-DD`); the desk cards (loss limit, open positions)
and the weekly focus only appear on today.

## Routines

A routine is your checklist, split into three stages — **Before trading**, **During trading**,
**After trading**. Open the editor from **Edit** on Today's routine card, `⌘K` → *Routines*, or
Settings → Rules → **Daily routine** → *Open Routines*.

- **Per-item days.** Each item runs on its own weekdays (*Every day*, *Weekdays*, or any set),
  so a Friday-only "weekly prep" item doesn't break Monday.
- **History.** **Last 13 weeks** shows each day's ticks, with **Completion**, **Streak** and a
  rate per stage. Click a past day to see or fix its ticks.
- **Streak** counts consecutive fully-done days. Days with no list (weekends on a weekday
  routine) neither count nor break it, and an unfinished today doesn't break it either.
- **Edits never rewrite history.** Removing an item, or changing its stage or days, applies from
  today on — past days keep what they were.
- **Ticks land on the market day.** A post-session tick after local midnight still counts
  toward the session it closed.

On mobile, Home carries a **Today's routine** card; **Daily routine** (Settings → Trading &
journal) shows the history and **Edit routine** the stage-sectioned editor. On iOS, **Sync to
Reminders** mirrors each item into the Reminders app as a repeating reminder on its own days,
due at one time you pick — completing it there ticks it in TraderMemos, and vice versa.

## Missed trades

Log the setups you saw and passed on: `⌘K` → *Missed trades* (`/missed`), the **Missed trades**
card on Today, or the Tools menu on mobile.

| Field | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| Symbol, Side, When you saw it | Required: symbol and side |
| Entry, Stop, Target | Optional plan; must be coherent for the side. With all three the miss is priced in R |
| Why you passed | Hesitated, Away from screen, Outside my rules, Other |
| How it played out | Not checked yet, Hit target, Hit stop, Never triggered — changeable inline from the list |
| Setup, Account, Notes | Optional |

Scoring: a target hit counts its planned R, a stop-out −1R, an untriggered entry 0R. The summary
shows **Would have made** (net R), **Left on the table** (target hits), **Losses avoided**
(stop-outs) and how many still need an outcome, broken down **By reason** and **By setup**.
Misses **never touch your trade stats**.

## iOS widgets, controls and Siri

The iOS app ships widgets and App Intents that work without opening the app:

- **Daily Routine** widget — small, medium and large Home Screen sizes plus Lock Screen
  (rectangular, circular, inline). Tap a row to tick or untick it straight to your server. If the
  server is unreachable the tick is queued and the widget redraws; the app sends the queue on
  its next launch. It refreshes every 30 minutes and at midnight.
- **Missed Trades** widget — small and Lock Screen: would-have-made R, logged count, and how
  many still need an outcome.
- **Shortcuts / Siri** — *Check Off Routine Item* (Siri asks which), *Check Off Next Routine
  Item*, *Routine Progress* (spoken), *Log Missed Trade* (asks symbol and side; reason and plan
  optional).
- **Control Center** (iOS 18) — *Next Routine Item* ticks the next open item; *Log Missed
  Trade* opens the form.
