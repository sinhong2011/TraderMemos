---
title: Weekly review
description: Grade every closed trade in the review inbox, get a pre-filled weekly review note on Saturday morning, and carry its focus into the next week.
---

The weekly loop has three parts: **grade** each closed trade while it's fresh, **review** the
week on Saturday, and **carry** one to three commitments into the next week.

## Review inbox

`/review` lists closed trades from the **last 14 days** that have no execution grade, newest
first, one at a time. A trade counts as reviewed once it has a grade — the lesson and mistakes
are optional.

| Control | Key | Effect |
|---------|-----|--------|
| **Execution grade** A+ · A · A- · B · C | `1`–`5` | Required to save |
| **Lesson** | — | One line, saved into the trade journal's *Review notes* section (other sections are kept) |
| **Mistakes** | — | Chips for your mistake tags; replaces only the trade's mistake tags |
| **Save & next** | `Enter` | Saves and moves on |
| **Skip** | `→` | Leaves the trade in the inbox |
| **Back** | `←` | Previous trade |

An empty queue shows **Inbox zero**. Ungraded trades older than the window are counted in the
header as "N older trades never reviewed" — **Dismiss** → *Stop counting them* clears that
backlog without grading anything (trades that age out later form a new one).

Entry points: "N trades to review →" in Today's **Session summary**, and *Open the review
inbox →* on the **Mistake tax** card's unreviewed-losers prompt. The **Unreviewed trades** alert
counts exactly the same trades (see [Alerts](/en/docs/guides/alerts-and-sharing)).

On mobile, Home shows "N trades to review" under the routine; it opens a queue — "Review 2 of
5", grade required, **Save and next**, **Skip for now** — ending on *Inbox zero*, *End of the
queue* or *Nothing to review*. The older backlog can only be dismissed on the web.

## Weekly review note

Every **Saturday at 09:00 in your market timezone** the server writes a review note titled
**"Week of …"** and sends a push / webhook summary that opens it on mobile. The note is
pre-filled:

- **Week in numbers** — net P&L, trades (won / lost), win rate, R, best and worst setup, rule
  breaks, missed trades
- **Last week's focus** — last review's focus as a checklist (when there was one)
- **What worked**, **What didn't**, **Focus for next week** — empty, for you to fill in

Turn it on or off under Settings → Rules → **Weekly review** (alerts must be enabled). It's
created once per week; a restart reuses it. The schedule is driven by
`TM_JOB_WEEKLY_REVIEW_INTERVAL_MIN` — see [Configuration](/en/docs/self-hosting/configuration).

## Weekly focus

Write up to **three bullets** under **Focus for next week** (plain lines under the heading are
ignored; only list items count). They become:

- **This week's focus** — a card at the top of Today on the web and on mobile Home, linking back
  to the review.
- **Last week's focus** — a checklist in the next generated review. Tick `- [x]` what you kept.
- **Weekly focus** — the first card on Reports → **Behavior** (web only): items kept, weeks fully
  kept, and each week's items with ✓ / ○. Click a week to open its review.

A focus older than two weeks isn't revived: skip a review and the card simply disappears.
