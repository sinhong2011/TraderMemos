Reports (`G` `S`) and Playbook (`G` `P`) answer three questions: **which setups to size up,
keep testing or stop**, **what your mistakes cost**, and **whether you're trading better or
worse than your own baseline**. Everything follows the filters at the top — date range,
accounts, side — and the market timezone.

## Setup scorecard (Playbook)

Each play gets a **verdict**, scored on the selected range. Stats are in R when at least 60% of
a setup's trades have an initial risk recorded; otherwise in currency.

| Verdict | When |
|---------|------|
| **Edge** | 20+ trades and the 95% interval sits above zero — size up within your risk rules |
| **Execution leak** | Losing overall, but its clean trades (10+) pay — the losses come from trades that broke your process |
| **Promising** | Positive so far, not proven yet |
| **Watch** | Negative so far, not enough trades to call it |
| **Too early** | Fewer than 10 trades (shown as e.g. *Too early · 6/10*) |
| **Bleeding** | 20+ trades and the interval sits below zero — stop or rework it |

- The **Edge** column draws expectancy per trade as a dot (the mean) with a whisker (the 95%
  interval) on an axis centred on zero. Rows in the same unit share one scale.
- **Without mistakes: +1.00R** appears when the clean expectancy differs noticeably. "Clean"
  drops every trade the mistake tax flags, not just mistake-tagged ones.
- **Not traded in 60+ days** marks stale setups.
- **Proven edge** in the summary reads e.g. *1 of 6*, with bleeding / too-early counts beneath.
- A **No setup** line at the bottom summarises trades logged without one.
- The list sorts by verdict by default; Name, Trades, Win rate, Expectancy and Net P&L sort too.

## Mistake tax (Reports → Overview)

The first Overview card prices the trades that broke your own process. A trade is flagged by
any of:

- a **mistake tag** you applied (*Chased*, *Moved stop*…)
- a broken **risk rule** — over max risk per trade, past the daily loss limit, over max trades
  per day, past the losing-streak limit (set under Settings → Rules)
- a detected **revenge trade** or **oversized after a win streak** entry

The card shows the total cost (losses only — a flagged winner never lowers it, it's listed as
"won anyway"), its **share of the range's losses**, the cost in R, and **Without them: … instead
of …** — your net had those trades not happened. Below: a weekly or monthly trend, then one row
per reason; expand a row to list its trades and click one to open it. A trade with several
reasons counts under each, so rows can add up to more than the total.

When losers have neither a mistake tag nor a note, a prompt lists them and links to the
[review inbox](/en/docs/guides/weekly-review). Today's session summary carries a one-line
version ("Mistake tax: $280.00 · Past daily loss limit…", or "clean session").

<Callout type="info">
Saved Reports presets keep the cards they were saved with. A preset saved before the mistake
tax existed won't show it until you switch it on under **Cards**.
</Callout>

## Edge Score

A 0–100 composite on Reports → Overview (and the mobile Reports overview). Each component scores
0–100 against a fixed target and is weighted:

| Component | Weight | Full marks |
|-----------|--------|------------|
| Profit factor | 25% | 3.0 |
| Avg win / loss | 20% | 2.5 : 1 |
| Win rate | 15% | 60% |
| Max drawdown | 15% | 0% of capital (25% scores 0) |
| Consistency | 15% | best day ≤ 15% of winning-day profit |
| Recovery | 10% | net P&L 3× max drawdown |

It needs **at least 5 closed trades** in range and no journaled risk or MAE/MFE.

## Rolling performance (Reports → Win / Loss)

Switch between **Win rate** and **Avg P&L** over a rolling window of 10, 20, 50 or 100 trades.
The latest value is compared against the baseline across every trade in range (*Above* /
*Below*). Available on mobile too.

## Everything else

- **Overview** — summary metrics with expectancy, profit factor, Kelly % (and half Kelly), SQN;
  period returns, execution quality score, playbook & leaks, R-multiple performance, execution
  grade
- **Win / Loss** — rolling performance, metric evolution
- **Detailed** — session clock, symbol & tag, day of week & hourly, win/loss bars, duration
  scatter, P&L heatmap, sessions, symbol heatmap
- **Risk** — risk & drawdown, Monte Carlo simulation, rule compliance
- **Behavior** — weekly focus, revenge trading, overconfidence, loss aversion

Trade drawers show MAE/MFE excursion charts. **Cards** shows, hides and reorders cards per tab;
**Presets** saves the current filters and card layout as a named view.
