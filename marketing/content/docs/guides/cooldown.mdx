---
title: Cooldown
description: A circuit breaker against revenge trading — a timed pause with a return gate, started by you or by your own risk rules.
---

A cooldown is a timed pause before the next trade. While one is open, **trade entry is
locked** — the web app veils the trade log and redirects "new trade" to the cooldown
panel, the mobile app locks its trade form — while reading your journal stays free.
The lock is on acting, not looking.

Ending a cooldown takes more than waiting: the **return gate** asks three questions —
what was pulling you in (revenge, FOMO, boredom, fear, proving yourself), the one
playbook setup you're allowed on return, and your rule for the rest of the day
(*half size*, *one trade*, or *done for today*). Unlocking **before** the timer ends is
allowed, but only with a written reflection of at least 40 characters. The typing is
the friction.

## Starting one

- **Web** — the **Cooldown** button on the Trades toolbar (or `/trades?panel=cooldown`).
  The tab title keeps counting (`⏸ 12:34`) so the clock follows you to other tabs.
- **Mobile** — Cooldown in the Home tools menu, or the Trades **+** while locked.
- **Automatically** — set **Auto cooldown** under **Settings → Rules**: when your daily
  loss, loss streak, or trades-per-day rule trips, the server starts the cooldown itself
  and notifies every registered device. Your journal knows you just took three losses —
  it doesn't wait to be asked.

## The promise has consequences

The return rule you pick is scored afterwards:

- **Rule compliance** (Reports → Risk) counts trades taken against a return rule on the
  day it was made.
- **Cooldowns** (Reports → Behavior) compares trades opened within an hour of a release
  against trades opened within an hour of a loss streak on days you did *not* cool down —
  the one number that says whether the pauses are working.

## On iOS

A **Live Activity** keeps the countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, so
the clock is there without opening the app — and when the timer ends it flips to "answer
the return gate" on its own.
